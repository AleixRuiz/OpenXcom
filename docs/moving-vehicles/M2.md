# Moving Vehicles — M2: Combat and AI drivers

Last updated 2026-10-29 · Aleix Ruiz Sorribes

M2 is the milestone that turns driving into a tank battle ([design](DESIGN.md#roadmap)): vehicles run over infantry, draw reaction fire, and fight vehicles driven by the AI. It ends with the convoy interception showcase, and is built before the pitch ([upstream strategy](DESIGN.md#upstream-strategy)), on top of the [MVP](MVP.md) and without changing it in passing, so the core can still be offered alone.

## What the player can do

- Run enemies over with a vehicle that has `runOverDamage`, by clicking so the vehicle ends on them; a unit that survives stops the vehicle.
- Drive past enemies and draw their reaction fire on the crew that shows: roof crew, turrets, open hatches.
- Fight enemy vehicles: a UFO whose map is marked as vehicles drives, with its own crew or mounted guns at the wheel.
- Watch the AI drive: it holds while its crew has targets, closes in on enemies it knows of, and runs over what stands in its way.
- Get more out of a vehicle with a skilled driver, and more still with the pilots assigned to the craft.
- Play Rosigma's traitor convoy as a moving convoy that tries to drive off the map.

## Decisions taken for M2

- **Running over is the player's choice.** The player's route goes around units, except the ones under the place clicked; AI drivers drive through enemies when it's the way ([GD-21](GAME_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md#gd-21--running-over-is-the-players-choice-ai-drivers-dont-hesitate)).
- **The hull is cover.** Reaction fire goes to the crew enemies can see, never to the hull ([GD-22](GAME_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md#gd-22--reaction-fire-against-everyone-on-board-who-shows)).
- **Enemy drivers come from the crew.** No units are added; one of the deployed enemies takes each seat, dedicated ranks first ([GD-27](GAME_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md#gd-27--enemy-vehicles-get-their-driver-from-the-deployed-crew)).
- **Parked until it knows of an enemy.** An AI vehicle with no known enemy stays where it is; only convoys drive without one ([GD-24](GAME_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md#gd-24--ai-drivers-hold-advance-or-stay-parked)).
- **The convoy rules are settled after the AI has been played** ([GD-25](GAME_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md#gd-25--convoys-drive-for-the-map-edge) stays proposed until Phase 12).

## Demo mods

- **Rosigma:** the Chimeras and the Baneblade get `runOverDamage`; the traitor convoy (`STR_TRAITOR_CHIMERA`, three Chimeras on one UFO map) becomes three enemy vehicles, each driven by a traitor put on its seat when the battle starts.
- **Vanilla:** the Skyranger gets `runOverDamage`, to show running over and reaction fire without Rosigma.

## Done when

- [ ] A vehicle with `runOverDamage` kills or knocks out the units it drives onto, credits the driver, and is stopped by a survivor; one without it is blocked as before.
- [ ] Crew showing on a driving vehicle draw reaction fire, and a shot stops the drive; crew inside a closed hull don't.
- [ ] The Rosigma traitor convoy's Chimeras are enemy vehicles that the AI drives, holding while their turrets have targets and closing in otherwise.
- [ ] Each enemy vehicle with seats starts the battle with one of its side's deployed units on a seat, dedicated ranks first, and no extra units.
- [ ] The convoy drives for the map edge, and the player can stop it by blocking the road, ramming or boarding.
- [ ] A skilled driver and an assigned pilot get further than a novice in the same vehicle.
- [ ] Battles saved mid-turn with player and enemy vehicles load with every vehicle where it was.
- [ ] Every new ruleset key is described in the design document, ready for the OXCE wiki.
- [ ] The Linux build (GCC with OXCE's warnings) has no warnings in our code. Passed for Phases 8 to 11 and 13 on 2026-10-29, also on macOS (Clang) and 32-bit Windows; to run again after Phase 12.
